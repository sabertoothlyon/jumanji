JUMANJI

A game for two players. Roll the dice, follow the path, and reach the Golden City.

Do not begin unless you intend to finish. The game will not end until one player reaches Jumanji.

How to play

Peter and Judy take turns rolling two six-sided dice and moving along the 42-space path. Landing on a named space triggers its event and printed consequence. A missed turn is skipped. Backward movement is applied immediately.

The shortcut grants another roll. Reach the Jumanji circle to win, then declare the name of the city aloud to end the game.

Reconstruction note: The book reveals only selected spaces. Unspecified spaces are represented as quiet, unmarked spaces. Their exact wording and positions are not documented here. The placement of the eight hazards and shortcut beyond the known first lion space is an illustrative game-design choice.
